# Vietnamese food list for curated-vn.json (v1, ~400 items)

Format per line: `Name (vi) | aliases (comma-sep: no-diacritic vi, english) | default serving (vi + ~gram hint) | notes`

Aliases: always include the no-diacritic Vietnamese form and an English term where one exists.
Serving: the natural Vietnamese unit with an approximate gram/ml weight; every food also gets a
`100 g` fallback serving in the JSON (not repeated here).
`source` in the JSON: `label` (packaged/chain published), `nin` (composition table), or
`recipe-estimate` (derived from a standard recipe).
